Good to know

Questions, answered

How much does window cleaning cost?

Every home is different, so we give a clear, free price up front based on the number and size of your windows — no surprises and no pushy sales calls. Message us on WhatsApp or call 083 875 0886 for a fast quote.

What areas do you cover?

We cover every town and village within roughly 40km of F26 W1Y6 — including Ballina, Foxford, Crossmolina, Enniscrone, Killala, Swinford, Ballycastle, Bonniconlon, Easkey, Bangor Erris, Tubbercurry, Charlestown and Dromore West across north Mayo and west Sligo. See all areas →

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry public liability insurance as standard and use safe pure-water poles instead of leaning ladders against your walls.

Do you clean gutters?

We clean facia and soffit — removing grime, cobwebs, dirt and algae for a fresh, bright finish. We don't offer gutter clearing at this time.

How often should windows be cleaned?

Most homes look their best with a clean every 4 to 8 weeks, but we're happy to do one-off cleans or a schedule that suits you.

How do I book?

Tap Get a free quote to send your details on WhatsApp, or call 083 875 0886. We offer same-week slots and text you the morning of your clean.
